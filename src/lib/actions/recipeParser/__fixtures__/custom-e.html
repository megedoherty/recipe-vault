Orange Marmalade Buns

Ingredients

Dough

  • 4 1/2 cups (540g) Acme Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
  • 3/4 cup (171g) milk, whole preferred, lukewarm (90°F to 100°F)
  • 6 tablespoons (75g) vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons (63g) honey
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 large egg yolk, white reserved
  • 4 teaspoons (12g) instant yeast
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons (9g) table salt

Filling

  • 1/3 cup plus 1 tablespoon (85g) dark brown sugar or 1/3 cup plus 1 tablespoon light brown sugar, packed
  • 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 teaspoons unsweetened cocoa, Dutch-process or natural
  • 1 teaspoon espresso powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 egg white, reserved from above
  • 1 cup (177g) mini chocolate chips or semisweet chocolate, very finely chopped

Glaze

  • 1/2 cup (113g) water
  • 1/2 cup (99g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon table salt
  • zest of 1 orange
